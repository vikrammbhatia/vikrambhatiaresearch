Entry — what the scan does
1. Market dip: Nifty ≥2% below its 20-day high. Otherwise no recommendation.
2. Causes: Claude searches recent news for the factors behind the fall (oil, rates, FII/rupee, trade, credit, domestic, pandemic).
3. Sectors not hit: sectors those causes do not touch. The cause-to-sector map is an approximation.
4. Stocks: Nifty 100 names in those sectors ≥5% below their own 20-day high.
5. Excluded: stocks that fell after an unusual run-up, weak fundamentals, or stock-specific bad news.
6. Picks: up to 3, one per sector, higher-quality names first.
Exit
Time exit: sell about 90 days after entry.
No stop, no target: tight stops and targets lost money in testing, even on random entries.
Your call: you can exit early on news that changes the company's outlook. Don't average down.
Sizing
Per position: ₹5,00,000
Max positions: 3, at most one per sector
Net P&L: after brokerage and statutory charges, and 20.8% tax on profit at sale (no loss offset)
