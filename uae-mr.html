UAE MR Strategy Rules
Entry Rules
Pullback: Stock down 6–18% from 20D high
Momentum: RSI below 38 (oversold)
Trend: Price above 200D MA (uptrend intact)
Volume: Current volume ≥1.5× 20D avg volume
Earnings: No results within next 7 days
Banking cap: Max 2 bank stocks per cycle
Penny filter: Price above AED 0.50
Liquidity: Avg daily value ≥AED 500K
Exit Rules
Target: +6% gross → sell all at market open
Stop loss: −4% gross → sell all immediately
Time stop: 21 days → exit regardless of price
Hard rule: All exits are non-negotiable
No averaging: Never add to a losing position
All-or-nothing: No partial exits permitted
Reward/Risk: 1.5:1 ratio (6% target / 4% stop)
Execution & Corpus
Broker: Emirates NBD (ENBD X app)
Corpus: AED 25,000 per cycle
Per position: AED 5,000 (max 5 positions)
Brokerage: ~0.15% per leg (~0.30% round trip)
Net target: ~+5.6% after costs
Net stop: ~−4.3% after costs
Markets: DFM and ADX
Macro check: Before each new cycle
Why 6% target / 4% stop for UAE?
UAE markets (DFM/ADX) are less liquid and more volatile than NSE. Stocks can gap sharply on thin volumes. A tighter stop (4% vs 5% in India) protects against gap-down risk. The target (6% vs 8% in India) is more achievable in UAE's shorter mean-reversion windows. The 1.5:1 payoff ratio combined with a target win rate of 70%+ generates positive expectancy of ~AED 100–150 per trade.
