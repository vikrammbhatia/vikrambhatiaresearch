Entry Rules — All Must Be Met
1. Pullback: Stock down 8-20% from 20-day high
2. Oversold: RSI below 35
3. Uptrend: Price above 200-day moving average
4. Volume: Today's volume ≥1.5× 20-day average
5. Earnings clear: No earnings in next 10 days
6. VIX filter: VIX below 30 (avoid high volatility)
Exit Rules — First Triggered Wins
Target hit: Stock up +8% from entry → Sell all (+$160 gross / +$152 net)
Stop loss: Stock down -5% from entry → Sell all (-$100 gross / -$108 net)
Time stop: 30 days elapsed → Sell regardless of P&L
Earnings: Exit before earnings announcement
No averaging down: Never add to losing position
